How to use Flexi-Time

Welcome to Flexi-Time! Here's how to get started:

Adding Activities

Click the + Add Activity button to create a new activity. Give your activity a title and set a daily target in minutes, then select a category.

2. Starting the Timer

Click the Start button on any activity card to begin tracking your progress. You can switch between activities whenever you want, this isn't a fixed timetable.

3. Stopping Activities.

Click the Pause button to stop the currently running activity, you can use this if you need to take a break.

4. Tracking Your Daily Progress

At the top of your screen, there is a daily summary. The daily summary shows your daily target, completed time, remaining time, and progress.

5. The Next Day

Your activities are saved, meaning you can close the tab and continue tommorow. Every day, your completed time will reset to 0, but the activities you created will remain.

Tip: Flexi-Time is a flexible alternative to a timetable, choose what to work on first based on your priorities, instead of following a rigid schedule.
